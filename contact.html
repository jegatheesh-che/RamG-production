Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is RamG Production based & do you travel?

RamG Production is based in Belgium and regularly documents weddings, portraits, and films across Europe (France, Netherlands, Germany, Italy, UK) and worldwide luxury destination venues.

How far in advance should we book?

To ensure date availability for peak European wedding seasons (May through October), we advise reaching out 6 to 12 months in advance.

What is the turnaround time for galleries and films?

You will receive a sneak peek highlights gallery within 48 to 72 hours following your event. Your full, high-resolution print archive and 4K film are delivered within 4 to 6 weeks.
